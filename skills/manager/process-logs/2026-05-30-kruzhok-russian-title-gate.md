## Durable rule

Founder-facing issue titles are Russian by default:

```text
{узнаваемый объект} — {конкретное действие} ({дата/контекст})
```

Allowed English: product names, brands, repo names, commands, APIs, labels, handles, and stable technical tokens such as `GitHub`, `Telegram`, `YouTube`, `LMS`, `OpenClaw`, `Personal Corp`, `Colvir`, `Hermes`, `LLM`, `PRD`, `E2E`, `video_id`.

Disallowed in title action text: English generic work words and service phrases such as `delivery track`, `launch/funnel`, `follow-up`, `workshop prep`, `handoff`, `rollout package`, `topic TBD`, `date TBD`.
